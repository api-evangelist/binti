Fifty-seven licensed foster homes exist for every 100 children in foster care nationwide. A Home for Every Child focuses on three operational priorities: licensing capacity, early kin engagement, and effective prevention referrals. Together, these day-to-day practices shape whether children can stay safely with family, move more quickly to permanency, or avoid foster care altogether.
